About

I am a geographer from Bangladesh working on the human side of natural hazards. My field research in Kurigram, Bogura and Sirajganj districts looks at how riverbank erosion and floods reshape livelihoods, and what makes char and riverine communities more resilient. I combine household surveys and participatory methods (KIIs, FGDs, PRA) with GIS mapping and resilience-index analysis, and I have worked as a field researcher on a JSPS-funded international project.

Outside research I write Bengali poetry, serve as Associate Editor (Fiction) of the literary magazine Kothar Kagoj, and photograph nature, streets and people.
